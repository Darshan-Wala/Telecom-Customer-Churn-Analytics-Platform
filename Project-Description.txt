Real World Problem

A telecom company (like Airtel, Jio, Verizon, AT&T) loses thousands of customers every month.

Acquiring a new customer costs much more than retaining an existing one.

The business wants answers like:

Which customers will leave?
Why are they leaving?
Which customers generate the highest revenue?
How much revenue will be lost if they churn?
Which customers should the retention team contact first?

Instead of manually analyzing Excel sheets, your platform predicts churn and provides actionable business insights.
